Sheet 01 Who we are

Young leaders building change that lasts

TYAC is a youth-led alliance working across Montserrado County, Liberia. We bring young people together around six things that shape everyday life — education, sanitation, youth policy, community impact, peacebuilding and mental health — and turn that energy into practical work in schools and neighbourhoods.

Sheet 02 Programme

Our Core Pillars

The core strategic focus driving our grassroots mobilization.

01

Quality Education

We get books, learning materials and better facilities into local schools, and run programmes that keep young people learning.

02

Environmental Sanitation

We organise community clean-ups, cut waste at the source, and hold our neighbourhoods accountable for the places we live in.

03

Policy Advocacy

We put young voices in the rooms where decisions get made — from local governance to national youth policy.

04

Community Impact Goals

With each community we agree clear, measurable goals — then keep working until the change is real and stays.

05

Peacebuilding and Conflict Resolution

We settle disputes through dialogue and empathy, addressing the root causes of conflict so tensions don't return.

06

Mental Health and Well-being

We make room to talk about how we think, feel and cope with stress — emotional and social wellness, without stigma.

Sheet 03 The Record

About Our Alliance

Who we are, where we came from, and the people steering the work.

01

Our Mission & Vision

To build an inclusive space where young people have the skills, support and leadership framework to change their own communities — starting now, not later.

02

Our History

TYAC grew out of deep-rooted community volunteering into structured provincial operations. We run flagship school initiatives and regional sanitation exercises, and we stay on the issues that shape young people's livelihoods. The Youth Alliance For Change was founded on August 4, 2025 to make a difference.
